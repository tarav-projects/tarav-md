# Surface Presence Checks

Detect observed Agentos surfaces.

## Surfaces

- `AGENTS.md`
- `.agentos/`
- `notes/index/`
- `notes/live/`
- `skills/`
- `dev/live/`

## Rules

- Surface presence is evidence, not an automatic pass/fail.
- Compare observed surfaces to declared adoption and target context.
- Missing surfaces are warnings or skips unless the target declares or clearly depends on them.

## Report Evidence

List checked surfaces in the summary.

For each important missing surface, include:

- expected surface
- why it was expected
- observed related evidence
