## Index.Line/Grounding.Mechanics

## Index.Line/Grounding.Density

- [DC] `Grounding.Density` is the first grounding metric: unique inline anchors / kernel lines — evidence density relative to block size ^c20260709-3u ^c20260709-6u
- a fresh block-approval stamp reads 1/n — covered but coarse; the ratio may exceed 1 (up to 3 anchors per line); a resolution gauge, not a debt gauge ^c20260709-3u
- the metric aggregates over any scope — block, chapter, depot: unique inline anchors within the scope / kernel lines in the scope ^c20260709-5u
