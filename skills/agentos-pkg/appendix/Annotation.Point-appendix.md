## Annotation.Point/Appendix

- host-native examples: skw-md annotates markdown sections; skw-code annotates functions, classes, modules; every backend has the file ^20260711-5
- annotation sections add no content of their own — they exist to give annotations something to scope, and matter most where host divisions are coarse or absent ^20260711-5
- end-free sections are a deliberate carve-out from strict owned-end closure: dividers have no body whose extent needs proving — heading-closure semantics apply to them and only them ^20260711-5
- the point vocabulary is expected to grow with usage (`Specified.By.Precedence` — the built backends become the soft spec for the next) ^20260711-5
