## Independent.Reader.Block

- failure form one — ephemeral reference: a session id, date, note number, or "the session we checked" that cannot resolve outside the authoring moment
- failure form two — contrastive framing: "not just X", "this is also", "actually" — defining a thing by negating a framing the reader never saw
- contrastive framing is the subtle form: it reads fine to the author who recalls the framing, but answers a question the reader never saw asked
- positive restatement is the repair: replace "not just a gate, it is X" with "it is X"
- test: would this still land for someone who has only the block and the index it cites, never the chat? if it needs the conversation, rewrite as a standalone positive claim or cut it
- distinct from `Public.Surface` self-containment, which is independence from local project knowledge; `Independent.Reader.Block` is independence from the authoring conversation
- the hazard generalizes beyond the index — design notes, READMEs, commit messages distilled from a conversation carry the same deixis risk
