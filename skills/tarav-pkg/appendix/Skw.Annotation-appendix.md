## Skw.Annotation/Appendix

- the declaration is documentation that cannot silently rot — the checker watches its targets; understanding and maintenance are the same feature ^c20260711-47u
- two constraint tiers price differently: the declared tier (fragment vs annotation) is the continuously watched inner loop; the ambient tier (fragment vs the whole index and chat) holds as always via rarer agentic sweeps — annotations watch the hot subset, they do not replace the global obligation ^c20260711-59u
- annotation scope is editorial — the author regulates each annotation's size; granularity policy (block-hash vs line-level deps) is a parked `Experience.Gated.Decision` ^c20260711-19u

- [DC] `$kw` marks the fence, `--` fills it: `$kw:` opens and names the block by its `Skw.Address.Schema` address, `$kw. end` closes, and every line between is a self-marked `--flag` — the sigil rides the host carrier (blockquote in markdown, comments in code) only on the two fence lines ^c20260720-33u
- [DC] `--cont` continues the previous flag generically; a `--cont` before any flag is a finding ^c20260720-34u
- [DC] the body between the fences is the block's generated target; where it wraps a named host element, that element's name checks the declared address ^c20260720-45u
- [DC] a nameless span is named by a `^label` address, src-only and stripped at compile; renaming it breaks importers, caught by the dead-link check ^c20260711-63u
