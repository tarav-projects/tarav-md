## Cumulative Context views and typed steps

The User refined Context into a graph of navigable contextualization steps:
entries and values may specialize their Types, and several paths may reach
the same value with different meanings. Views accumulate qualifications on a
subject. `.ls()` offers supported next steps; `.open()` opens a view or a node.
The accepted interface is `obj.context(views=(SomeView, Request(...)))`, applied
left to right. Built under autonomous Develop as request 912; see
[Context Graph](../context-graph.md), "Context as cumulative contextualization".
