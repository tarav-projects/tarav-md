## TaravRecords implementation design

The discussion settled on `TaravRecords` as a workflow for reusable Record storage primitives and basic discovery and navigation across workflow-owned storage. The desired graph is built from hooks into those storage solutions, without a central entry for every Record. Archive retains its separate Artifact and long-term preservation meaning; a Registry is a way to store Records and may be used by Archive. Tarav can provide standard Registry locations while other workflows keep custom storage. The candidate implementation boundary and its open migration question are recorded in [Impl.Req 864](../../../../tarav-py/notes/live/864-tarav-records-workflow-impl-req.md) and [TaravRecords](../tarav-records.md).
