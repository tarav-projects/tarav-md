## PaperSON motivated a reusable Harness

The session began by clarifying PaperSON as a compiler from published evidence
into structured data whose quality improves through two fronts: constraints
that help Agents work well up front, and tests, POIs, audits, and review that
surface possible mistakes afterward. This became a motivating use case for
[Tarav.Harness](../tarav-harness.md), a reusable core for making systems
legible, operable, and self-evaluating for Agents.

## Harness mechanics were consolidated

The discussion separated several identity levels that use the same minimal
Ship primitive: a class can have a Type Ship while also modeling domain Ships,
and retained Records can carry their own Ships. It also established the
registered class as semantic authority over publicly inspectable but
semantically private JSON, with `Type.Resolution.PATH` used only when an opaque
retained Record needs dispatch. `assertions()` and `findings()` were selected
as the next shared evaluation capabilities to develop.

## Diagram was reframed around workflows

[Diagram workflows](../diagram-workflows.md) were reframed as an ecology of
situational capture workflows and broader reading/contextualization workflows
joined through Tarav.Harness and Tarav.Diagram. Each workflow is implemented
jointly by deterministic Python code and Agent skills; capture and discovery
may optimize their representations independently and converge through useful
interfaces over time.

## Project.Wiki capture was designed and corrected

The first wiki workflow was designed as `Capture.Note`: an additive,
User-Turn-grounded capture into topical Markdown Notes with scoped Git closure.
Its first trial bootstrapped the wiki with Notes for Harness, Diagram workflows,
and the capture workflow itself.
<!-- tarav.context 33afe59c:864b0f87dd82fb92 -->
<!-- tarav.context 33afe59c:f4fe73f3f878f979 -->

That trial exposed a mistaken interpretation of the daily Note as optional.
The design was corrected to two simultaneous views: a mandatory daily narrative
of relevant events and opportunistic topical integration. The workflow was
therefore renamed [`Capture.Wiki`](../capture-wiki.md); its capture boundary and
Git closure are now the complete Project.Wiki.
