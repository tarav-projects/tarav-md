## Batchy.Skill design recovered

The user requested inspection of the former tarav-pkg index in Tarav.Attic,
then capture into [Batchy skills](../batchy-skills.md). The recovered design
models a skill as an agent-interpreted CLI-like surface: flags, conversational
inference, and defaults resolve into one configuration; arguments, modules,
and routing have distinct responsibilities. The topical note records precedence,
composition, progressive loading, help, banners, and routing testability,
alongside the inspected Deposit and source locations. Historical conventions
are distinguished from current skill behavior rather than reinstated wholesale.

## Typed SKW components and Batchy specialization

The discussion developed a split between general SKW surfaces, built-in
`skw.skills`, and a separate `batchy_skills` application. SkillLink was clarified
as a typed consumer-facing component, potentially rendered by code through a
shared template, not an internal SKW dependency reference. The user endorsed
typed blocks deriving from a minimal SkwBlock and clarified that `.skw.json`
must retain their current output. After considering `return`, the field name
remains `output`. Captured in [Skillware](../skillware.md#typed-surface-components-and-retained-output)
and [Batchy skills](../batchy-skills.md#batchy-as-a-skillware-extension);
these are design decisions and proposals, not a completed implementation.
