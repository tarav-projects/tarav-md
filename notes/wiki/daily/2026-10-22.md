## Project integration belongs to managers

The User settled on ActionManager, ChecksManager, SkwManager and RecordsManager
as integration objects belonging to Project Classes and extensible by derived
Project Classes. Workflow primitives retain their core functionality. The User
clarified that loops also belong to managers, as one form of integration, and
requested Develop to create the managers and move existing integration there.
