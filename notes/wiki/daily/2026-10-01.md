## Workflow-owned POIs and blessings

[Impl.Req 833](../../../../tarav-py/notes/live/833-workflow-owned-pois-and-blessings-impl-req.md)
landed the workflow-owned POI migration. Producing workflows now define their
concrete POIs over the neutral shared foundation; the old kernel occurrence
marker and parallel POI occurrence/check model were removed.
<!-- tarav.breadcrumb df52568d00768328dde3dd1af56332eb79f6b7372bb86bc75b9fdfa28cad6d18 -->

Blessings became an independent workflow. Blessability is expressed only by
inheriting `BlessablePOI`, so ordinary POIs do not carry a runtime permission
flag. Check POIs and blessability compose by inheritance without taking
ownership away from the producing workflow.
<!-- tarav.breadcrumb ebc34e2f31d2448cca30655dc6d28af4efbe9ae6ffa3ceefc4e5e1bf191eeae7 -->

Archive also moved its existing skipped-remote notice onto shared typed
notification delivery. The operation still returns its artifact; callers see
one warning by default or may explicitly collect the notification.
<!-- tarav.breadcrumb 0feef49550451259b96a7651fe03956882c9db6bb5944715bb848e60b6549b6b -->

Focused verification passed 149 tests, including POI, Checks, Blessings,
References, Navigation, Skills, SKW, Archive, runtime contracts, Breadcrumbs,
and architecture. The broader repository run reached 1,304 passes; remaining
failures were the already disconnected Models surface and sandbox restrictions
on sockets, uv environment creation, cache access, and Git writes.
